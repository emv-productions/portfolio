About

I’m Ezequiel Vieta, a passionate photographer and videographer dedicated to visual storytelling.
As a digital nomad, I travel the world based on each project, always ready to explore new places and capture unique moments. My work is driven by the belief that every story deserves to be told in an authentic and visually compelling way.
What sets me apart in the creative industry is my distinct perspective and my ability to translate emotions and experiences into powerful imagery. For me, storytelling isn’t just about showcasing the highlights—it’s about capturing the in-between moments, the raw emotions, and the true essence of each experience.
I believe that great visuals go beyond aesthetics; they create connections, evoke emotions, and bring stories to life. Through my projects, I aim to craft narratives that feel immersive, natural, and deeply engaging.
Thank you for visiting my website and taking the time to explore my work. If you’re looking to collaborate or want to learn more about my creative approach, feel free to reach out—I’d love to connect.
🎥 Want to see more of my video work? Check out my YouTube and Instagram, where I share a variety of content, from cinematic reels to full-length productions.
